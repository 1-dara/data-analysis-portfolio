## 📁 Foundational Projects (In this repository)
These projects demonstrate my ability to handle data at each stage of the analytics lifecycle.

### 1. Excel: Business Intelligence & Pivots (`/excel`)
- **Goal:** Perform initial data audits and build executive summaries.
- **Outcome:** Analyzed Superstore data to find that the Central region had a profit margin half that of the West, and identified Furniture as a loss-making category.

### 2. SQL: Business Q&A (`/sql`)
- **Goal:** Extract insights from relational databases using advanced SQL.
- **Outcome:** Wrote a series of 8 queries (using `GROUP BY`, `JOIN`, `CTE`, and `CASE`) to prove that discounts above 40% consistently result in negative profit.

### 3. Python: Data Cleaning & EDA (`/python/superstore-python`)
- **Goal:** Clean raw data and perform statistical exploratory data analysis.
- **Outcome:** Converted text-based dates to datetime objects, engineered a "Days to Ship" feature, and generated scatter plots proving the direct correlation between high discounts and financial losses.

### 4. Tableau: Interactive Dashboards (`/tableau-dashboard`)
- **Goal:** Translate analytical findings into interactive, stakeholder-facing visuals.
- **Outcome:** Built and published a dashboard highlighting regional profit margins and sub-category losses. 
  - *[View the Live Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/irene.peter.okon/vizzes)*
